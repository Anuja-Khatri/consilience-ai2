Abstract. We trained the model on 1,200 images and report an accuracy of 87.5% on the held-out set. The effect may be associated with the larger batch size. Our analysis suggests that the regularisation could reduce over-
fitting. The ﬁnal score was 0.42 ± 0.03 across five seeds. Prior work reported a 12% gain in a related set-
ting, measured with “paired” comparisons.
